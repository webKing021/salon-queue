# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends customers sit in the salon for 1–2 hours without knowing when
their turn will come. Some get bored and leave, others argue about who came
first. The receptionist has no clear view of who is waiting for which barber.

## Users
- **Customer** — joins the queue from their phone (at home or in the salon) and watches their turn.
- **Receptionist / Owner** — runs the queue on a tablet: adds walk-ins, calls the next customer, marks no-shows.
- **Barber** — sees their current and next customer on their phone, taps Start / Done.
- **Waiting area** — a TV that shows who is with which barber and who is next.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from a phone: name, phone number, pick 1–3 services and a barber (or "Any barber").
2. Live status page: token number, position in line, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert and "It's your turn!" screen.
3. Reception dashboard: see all barbers and the waiting list, add walk-ins, Call next, No-show, Cancel.
4. Barber view: current customer, Start / Done buttons, next customer, On break / Available toggle.
5. Waiting-area TV display: each barber's current token + the next 5 tokens, flash and chime when a token is called.
6. Owner settings: services (name, minutes, price, on/off), barbers on duty, open/close online queue, today's stats.
7. All screens stay in sync across devices within 5 seconds, without refreshing.
8. English / Gujarati language toggle on every screen.

## Don't build
- Online payment
- Appointments for future dates (today's queue only)
- SMS / WhatsApp / email notifications
- Loyalty points, coupons, reviews
- Multiple branches
- Customer accounts / login (staff screens use a simple PIN only)

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal background with gold accents, rounded cards,
clean and mobile-first. All labels available in English and Gujarati.
